# Inklub Store Manager — How to Use

This is your guide to managing the Inklub app day to day.
No technical knowledge needed — everything can be done from the dashboard.

---

## Logging in

Open the app URL in your browser and enter your admin password.
You'll stay logged in until you close the browser or log out manually.

---

## Managing orders

### Adding a new order (placed via WhatsApp/DM)
1. Go to **Orders** and select the right market (Canada or Panama)
2. Tap **+ New order**
3. Fill in the customer name, product description, quantity, and price
4. Tap **Save** — the order appears as *Pending*

### Moving an order through production
Tap any order to open it, then update the status:
- **Pending** → you've received the order, not started yet
- **In production** → you're working on it (Cricut cutting, applying vinyl, etc.)
- **Ready** → finished and waiting for pickup/delivery
- **Delivered** → done! The order moves to the archive automatically

### Marking an order as paid
Tap the order and toggle **Payment status** to *Paid* once you receive the e-Transfer or Yappi payment.

### Customer order form (share this link with customers)
Instead of taking orders manually, send customers one of these links:
- **Canada:** `https://your-app.onrender.com/order/ca`
- **Panama:** `https://your-app.onrender.com/order/pa`

They fill in their name, product, size, and any customization notes.
The order lands in your dashboard automatically as *Pending*.

## Managing inventory

### Adding a new item
1. Go to **Inventory** and select the market
2. Tap **+ Add item**
3. Choose the category, enter the name, quantity, and low-stock threshold
4. Tap **Save**

### Updating quantity (after restocking)
Tap any item and update the quantity. If it drops to or below the threshold, you'll get an email alert automatically.

### Setting the low-stock threshold
Each item has its own threshold. Tap the item → edit threshold.
For example: set vinyl sheets threshold to 3 so you get an alert when only 3 sheets are left.

### Adding a new product category
1. Go to **Inventory**
2. Tap **+ category** in the filter bar
3. Type the category name (e.g. "Tote bags") and pick the unit (units, pcs, etc.)
4. Tap **Add** — the category is available immediately

### Removing a category
Tap the × next to any category chip. Note: you can only remove a category if no items are assigned to it. Move or delete those items first.

---

## Customer history

Go to **Customers** to see everyone who has placed an order.
Tap any customer to see all their past orders, total spent, and any notes you've added.
Use the notes field to save useful info like their usual size, preferred style, or Instagram handle.

---

## Settings

Go to **Settings** to update:
- **Notification email** — where low-stock alerts are sent
- **Default low-stock threshold** — the starting threshold for new items
- **Market labels** — currency and payment method names for each market

---

## Something looks wrong?

- **Order not showing up?** Refresh the page and check the correct market is selected.
- **Not receiving email alerts?** Check the notification email in Settings and make sure it's correct.
- **Can't log in?** Contact Alberto to reset the admin password in the server settings.
